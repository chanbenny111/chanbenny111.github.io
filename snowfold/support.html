Support

Fold a sheet, make a few cuts, unfold. If something gets in the way, the answer is probably below.

Purchases

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings (the gear at the top right of any tab) and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the Full Version page. A grown-up answers a short question first. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy Snowfold.

Does Family Sharing work?

Yes. The full version is a one-time purchase that can be shared with up to five other family members through Family Sharing. On their device, open Snowfold and tap Restore purchases if it isn't unlocked straight away. Purchase Sharing must be turned on in the organizer's Family Sharing settings.

What is free, and what does the full version add?

Free: chapters 1 and 2, every daily puzzle (including the Saturday big snowflake), puzzles friends send you, and half and quarter folds in the Studio and in Endless practice.

The full version unlocks chapters 3 to 6, eighth folds and hexagon snowflakes in the Studio and in Endless practice, and printing for every level. It is a single purchase: no subscriptions and no ads.

Playing

When do new daily puzzles appear?

At midnight in your device's own time zone. Everyone gets the same three puzzles for the same calendar date, plus a big snowflake on Saturdays.

If you travel across time zones, the day follows your device's clock, so you may get a new day a little early or late. Your streak includes a rest day each week, and you can catch up on any of the past 30 days from the Today tab (catch-up days don't extend your streak).

A level feels too hard.

Switch difficulty at any time in Settings. Little Hands shows a guide for every cut, always shows the preview and gives stars more easily; finishing the last level of a chapter also opens the next chapter. Changing difficulty never takes stars away.

Every cut can be undone, and after unfolding you can tap Fold back to keep cutting the same sheet. Press and hold the unfolded paper to lay the target over it and see what is different.

How do I open a puzzle a friend sent me?

Tap the snowfold:// link in their message. If the link doesn't open, copy it (or just the code), then in Snowfold go to Studio, tap the ⋯ menu at the top and choose Open a puzzle code…. Puzzles from friends are always free to play.

How do I print a puzzle?

Choose Print on the result card after a level, on a piece in the Collection, or on your sheet in the Studio. Snowfold makes a two-page PDF in A4 or US Letter: page 1 shows the numbered fold steps, page 2 the folded piece with its cut lines. Print at 100% (no "fit to page") so the folded shape matches the paper. Levels from chapters 1 and 2 print for free.

How do I add the widget or use Siri?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit then Add Widget, and choose Snowfold (small or medium). It shows today's puzzles and your streak; tap it to start a puzzle.

You can also say "Open today's puzzles in Snowfold" or "Open the Snowfold studio" to Siri, or find both actions in the Shortcuts app.

Accessibility

Which accessibility features does Snowfold support?
  • Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility sizes on every screen.
  • VoiceOver reads every screen, level and result, for example "3 of 3 stars, 86% match". You can fold with a VoiceOver action, and on Little Hands you can also cut along each guide with a VoiceOver action. Free cutting in the Studio is a drawing task and needs touch.
  • Reduce Motion replaces the unfolding animation with a short fade.
  • Dark Mode and Increase Contrast are followed automatically. Settings adds High-contrast paper and Slower animations.
  • A left-handed layout that moves the tools and the Unfold button to the left.
  • Connect the dots cutting: tap corner by corner instead of dragging a cut.
  • Results never rely on colour alone: stars are filled or outlined and read out as a number, and Compare marks extra cuts with stripes and missed cuts with dots.
