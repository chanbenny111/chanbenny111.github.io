Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

The Snowfold app collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you make stays on your device.

Our website is the one place where anything reaches us, and only when someone opens a page on it in a web browser. See “Puzzle links and our website” below.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress, stars, streak, saved pieces and any sheet you are halfway through cutting.
  • Your settings, such as difficulty, sound and layout, and whether the full version is unlocked (so the app opens unlocked even before the App Store answers).
  • The small amount of data the Home Screen widget needs (today’s puzzle and your streak), shared between the app and its widget on the same device.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or purchase history; the app only learns from Apple whether the full version is unlocked on your device.

Network and iCloud

Snowfold makes no network requests of its own and does not use iCloud. The only time it talks to the internet is when it asks the App Store for the price of the full version or checks your purchase, and Apple handles that connection.

Links that leave the app (this policy, the support page, the Terms of Use) open in your web browser after a grown-up answers a short question. From then on it is an ordinary visit to a website; see the next section.

Sharing, printing and photos

When you choose to share an image, a result line or a puzzle code, Snowfold opens the standard iOS share sheet. What you send, and to whom, is up to you; we don’t see your messages or who you send them to. A puzzle code and its QR code are made on your device and contain only the folds and cuts of a puzzle, nothing about you.

Printing uses the standard iOS print dialog. If you pick “Save Image” in the share sheet, iOS asks once whether Snowfold may add photos to your library. Snowfold can only add the picture you chose; it never reads your photos.

Snowfold asks for no other permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or notifications.

Puzzle links and our website

A shared puzzle travels as a link such as https://chanbenny111.github.io/snowfold/p/?c= followed by the puzzle code. The code is part of the page address itself (not a part after a “#” that stays in the browser), so when the person you sent it to opens the link in a web browser, their browser asks our website for that page, and the code reaches the web server along with the request. Some messaging apps also fetch the page by themselves to show a preview of the link. The same is true for any other page on our website, such as this one.

Like almost every web server, the one that hosts our website keeps standard access logs of those requests: the page address (including the puzzle code), the time, the IP address the request came from, and the browser’s own description of itself (its “user agent”) and the page it came from, if any. These logs are kept by our hosting provider for security and to keep the site running.

  • We do not use them for analytics, advertising or profiles, and we do not try to work out who anyone is.
  • Our pages set no cookies, run no analytics or tracking scripts, and load nothing from other companies.
  • The puzzle page only reads the code from its own address to show an “Open in Snowfold” button; it sends nothing anywhere.

The QR code on a puzzle card holds the same link, so scanning it with the Camera opens the same web page. If you would rather nothing reach a web server, copy the link and paste it into Studio in Snowfold instead of opening it in a browser; nothing is sent when you do that.

Children

Snowfold is made to be safe for children. It collects no personal information from anyone, shows no ads, and asks a grown-up to answer a short question before any purchase, restore, outside link, share or print. There are no accounts, no chat and no way to contact other players.
