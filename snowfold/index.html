Snowfold

Fold. Cut. Unfold.

A quiet paper-cutting puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Fold a sheet, cut a few shapes, then unfold it and see how close you came to the pattern.

Three new puzzles every day, six chapters to work through, and a studio where you can cut anything you like and send it to a friend as a puzzle. No account, no ads, no tracking.
