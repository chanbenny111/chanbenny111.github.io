Write it. Burn it. Let go.

A tiny ritual for big feelings. Write down what's making you angry, anxious or heartbroken, hold the button, and watch the page catch fire. Nothing you write is ever saved.

Download on the App Store

Why people burn it

Scream to Burn

Yell at your phone. The louder you are, the bigger the fire. The app only measures loudness and never records a sound.

Your burn, as a video

One tap makes a 9:16 video of the burn with its sound and your rage score, ready for TikTok, Reels or Stories. Your words stay scrambled unless you choose to show them.

Burn the photo

Burn their texts. Pick a photo or screenshot and send it up in flames. No photo library access, never saved. Your first one is free.

A fire that looks real

Rendered live on your iPhone's GPU with crackling, procedurally generated sound and haptics you can feel.

Stream-safe

While your screen is recorded, your words are blurred and scrambled. Share the fire, keep your secrets.

Unsent letters

Add a "To:" line and write the letter you'll never send. Then let it go.

Feel how much lighter

A quick mood check before and after shows how much lighter you feel on average.

Nothing is kept

No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Your words exist only until they burn.

Pro, once, forever

Fire, Scream mode with your rage score, burn videos (with a small watermark), one photo burn, Stream-safe, stats and widgets are free. A single purchase, with no subscription, unlocks clean videos without the watermark, unlimited photo burns, five more rituals (Shredder, Dissolve, Dust, Black Hole, Paper Plane), Sit by the fire (a campfire with a sleep timer and breathing guide), paper styles, soundscapes, every share card style, and full history and insights.

Let It Burn is a self-care ritual, not therapy or a medical service. If you are thinking about hurting yourself, please reach out to a local helpline. You can find one for your country at findahelpline.com.
