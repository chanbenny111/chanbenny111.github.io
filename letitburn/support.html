Support

Something not working, or an idea for a new ritual? Email chanbenny111@gmail.com. We read everything and usually reply within two working days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Need to talk to someone now? Let It Burn is not a crisis service. In the US, call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans on 116 123. Anywhere else, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines in more than 130 countries. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Frequently asked questions

Is what I write really gone?

Yes. Your words exist only in memory while you type and are wiped when the burn starts. They are never saved, backed up or uploaded. We couldn't recover them even if you asked.

Does Scream mode record me?

No. It reads only how loud the microphone input is, many times a second, to size the fire. No audio is recorded or stored. If you'd rather not grant microphone access, everything else works without it.

Scream mode doesn't react.

Check that microphone access is on in iOS Settings > Let It Burn. Scream mode works with every ritual. Bluetooth headsets with their own noise suppression can soften very loud input. Try the iPhone's built-in microphone.

How do I share a burn without anyone reading it?

Stream-safe mode is on by default. While your screen is being recorded, mirrored or streamed, your words are automatically blurred and scrambled. You can also turn on "Always Stream-safe" in Settings.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought Pro with. Pro can also be shared with your Family Sharing group.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The widget shows the wrong streak.

Open the app once. The widget refreshes after every burn and at midnight. If it still looks wrong, remove and re-add the widget.

How do I erase my stats?

Settings > Erase stats deletes your counts, streak and mood history from the device.
