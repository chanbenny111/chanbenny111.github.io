How it works

Pick. Line up. Snap.

  1. Pick a scene

    84 scenes and counting: heroes, pets, weddings, Halloween, holidays, pranks and more — illustrated and photo-real.

  2. Line up your face

    The scene sits over your live camera, so what you see is what you get. Front or back camera, one face or a whole group.

  3. Snap and share

    Save the photo, or share a short reveal video that drops the scene onto your face. Share it anywhere, Instagram Stories included.

What’s inside

Small details, big laughs

Prank with a friend’s photo

Pick any photo from your library. On-device face detection lines the face up with the hole for you — nudge, pinch or rotate to fine-tune.

Make it say anything

Wanted posters, magazine covers and newspapers have text you can rewrite: a name, a reward, a headline.

Blends right in

Eight filters and a one-tap blend help your face match the scene’s light and colour.

Send a scene to a friend

Every shared photo carries a link to the same scene, so whoever sees it can try it in one tap.

Private by design

Your photos never leave your phone.

Every photo is composed on your device. Face detection uses the system’s own detector (Apple Vision on iOS, Android’s built-in detector), only to place the photo — nothing is identified, stored or uploaded. FaceScene uses Google Firebase for usage stats and crash reports and Google AdMob for ads, and asks before personalizing ads. Read the privacy policy.
