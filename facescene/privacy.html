FaceScene Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

FaceScene lets you put your face into illustrated scenes — a superhero, a lion, a wedding couple — right in your camera view. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: your photos never leave your device.

Your photos

Photos you take or pick from your library are processed entirely on your device. We do not upload, store or view them. Finished photos are kept in the app's private storage ("My works") until you delete them, and are saved to your photo library only when you tap Save. The app asks for add-only access to your library and uses the system photo picker, so it cannot browse your library.

Ads and tracking

On iOS, the app asks for permission before using your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time: in the app under Settings > Ad privacy choices (shown where this consent applies), and for tracking permission in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS). Subscribers to FaceScene Pro see no ads.

Camera

The camera is used only to show the live view and take photos while you are using the app.

Face detection

To line your face up with the hole in a scene automatically, the app looks for faces in the photo you just took or picked. This runs entirely on your device using the operating system's built-in face detection (Apple Vision on iOS, Android's system face detector). The app only uses the position and size of each face to move the photo; it does not identify anyone, does not create face templates or biometric identifiers, and does not store, upload or share any face data. The result is discarded as soon as your photo is created.

Children

FaceScene is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Photos in "My works" stay on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Analytics data is retained by Google for up to 14 months. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, contact us at the address below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and adjust the date above.
