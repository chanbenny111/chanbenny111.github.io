Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Repling collects nothing. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and the app makes no network connections at all. Camera frames are processed on your device and never leave it.

The camera

Repling needs the front camera to count your reps. Each frame is passed to Apple's on-device Vision framework, which reads the position of your joints. The app uses those positions to count reps and to place your creature beside you, then discards the frame.

  • Camera images are never saved, never uploaded, and never shown to anyone but you, live, on your screen.
  • The microphone is never used. The app does not request microphone access.
  • If you decline camera access, you can still count reps by hand with the Add one button.

Workout clips

When Record workouts is on in Settings, the app keeps a light recording of your workout in memory: your joint positions, your creature's position and the rep count. It does not keep camera images. After the workout, it uses those values to draw a 15–30 second vertical clip of your silhouette and your creature, with music the app generates itself.

The clip is written to the app's own storage on your device. It goes somewhere else only when you choose: through the standard iOS share sheet, or to your photo library.

Your photo library

Repling asks for add-only access to Photos, and only when you save a clip (by tapping Save to Photos, or by turning on Save clips to Photos). Add-only access lets the app put a clip into your library. It cannot see, read or change anything already there. If you decline, clips stay in Repling and you can still share them.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your creature — growth points, totals for each exercise, stage, bond and mood.
  • Your workout history — date, exercise, rep count, reps added by hand, and duration.
  • Your settings — daily goal, default exercise, sound, spoken rep count, and recording options.
  • A calibration reference for your camera spot (a few numbers describing your standing posture as the camera sees it, not an image).

We have no servers, so there is no copy of this data anywhere else. It is not sent to us, not shared with anyone, and not readable by other apps. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your device, under your control, like any app's data.

What the app does not do

  • No personal data is collected, not even anonymously.
  • No identifiers are read or created. The app never requests tracking permission and never touches the advertising identifier.
  • No microphone, location, contacts, health data, notifications or Game Center access is requested.
  • No third-party SDKs, crash reporters, analytics or advertising networks are included.

Children

Repling collects no data from anyone, of any age, and contains no chat, no user-generated content shared with others, and no links out to the web.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored, permanently. Clips you saved to Photos or shared elsewhere are yours and stay where you put them; delete them there. There is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask you first.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 7 日

练练宠不收集任何东西。没有账号、没有广告、没有统计、没有追踪，App 完全不联网。摄像头画面只在你的设备上处理，不会离开这台设备。

摄像头

练练宠需要前置摄像头来数你的动作。每一帧画面交给 Apple 在设备本地运行的 Vision 框架，读出你关节的位置；App 用这些位置来计数、把搭档摆在你身边，然后把这一帧丢掉。

  • 摄像头画面从不保存、从不上传，除了你自己在屏幕上实时看到，没有任何人能看到。
  • 从不使用麦克风，App 也不申请麦克风权限。
  • 如果你拒绝摄像头权限，仍然可以用「Add one」按钮手动计数。

训练短片

设置里打开「Record workouts」后，App 会在内存里记下这一轮训练的简要数据：你关节的位置、搭档的位置和计数，不保留任何摄像头画面。训练结束后，App 用这些数据画出一条 15–30 秒的竖版短片，内容是你的剪影和搭档，配乐由 App 自己生成。

短片保存在 App 自己的本机存储里。只有你自己选择时，它才会去别的地方：通过 iOS 系统分享面板发出，或者存进你的相册。

相册

练练宠只申请相册的「仅添加」权限，而且只在你保存短片时申请（点「Save to Photos」，或打开「Save clips to Photos」）。仅添加权限只能把短片放进相册，看不到、读不到、也改不了相册里原有的任何内容。如果你拒绝，短片会留在 App 里，照样可以分享。

App 在本机保存什么

  • 你的搭档：成长值、各动作累计数、进化段、亲密度和心情。
  • 训练记录：日期、动作、个数、手动补加的个数、时长。
  • 设置项：每日目标、默认动作、声音、语音报数、录制选项。
  • 机位校准基准：几项描述摄像头所见站姿的数值，不是图像。

我们没有服务器，所以这些数据在别处不存在任何副本：不会发给我们、不会分享给任何人，其他 App 也读不到。它可能随你自己的 iCloud 或电脑设备备份一起被备份，由你掌控，和所有 App 的数据一样。

App 不做的事

  • 不收集任何个人数据，匿名的也不收。
  • 不读取、不生成任何标识符；从不请求追踪授权，也从不接触广告标识符。
  • 不申请麦克风、定位、通讯录、健康数据、通知和 Game Center 权限。
  • 不含任何第三方 SDK、崩溃统计、分析工具或广告网络。

儿童

练练宠不从任何年龄的任何人那里收集数据，没有聊天、没有对他人公开的用户生成内容，也没有对外网页链接。

删除数据

删除 App 即可永久删除它保存的全部数据。你存进相册或分享出去的短片归你所有，留在你放的地方，请在那里删除。我们这边没有任何需要删除的东西。

变更

若将来某个版本确实需要收集什么，本页会在该版本发布之前更新，并且 App 会先征求你的同意。
