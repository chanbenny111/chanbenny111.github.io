Train safely

Repling counts reps; it is not a coach and not a medical device. Stop if you feel dizzy or in pain. If you are injured, pregnant, or have a heart condition, ask a doctor before you start. The same note sits in Settings › Training inside the app.

Where to put your phone

  • Squats — stand the phone up at about knee height, two steps away, facing you. A rep counts when your hips drop to about knee height and you stand back up.
  • Jumping jacks — same spot as squats, with room above your head in the frame. A rep counts when your hands pass over your head, your feet jump wide, and you come back together.
  • Push-ups — lay the phone flat on the floor beside you, screen up, about an arm's length away. Do two full push-ups first so the app learns your range; after that, every rep down to that depth counts.

Common questions

A rep didn't count.

Repling never drops a rep silently. When an attempt was close but not complete, it says why on screen — Go a little lower, Jump your feet wider, Hands all the way over your head — and your creature shakes its head. If counting still goes wrong, tap Add one; the summary marks how many reps were added by hand.

It keeps saying "Step back" or "Too dark".

The app needs to see your whole body, including your feet. Step back until the hint says Perfect — hold still, tilt the phone down if your ankles are cut off, and turn on a light in front of you rather than behind you.

I said no to the camera.

Go to Settings → Repling → Camera on your iPhone and turn it on, or keep training with Count manually instead. Frames are only ever processed on your device.

My clip didn't save to Photos.

Photos access is probably off. Turn it on in Settings → Repling → Photos, or share the clip straight from the clip screen — it stays in Repling either way.

Why does my creature look different from my friend's?

What you train shapes it. Squats grow the legs, jumping jacks the arms, push-ups the wings. Two people who train differently end up with different creatures.

I missed a few days. Is it gone?

No. It never dies and never loses growth. It just gets a little sleepy, and one workout wakes it up again.

Where is my progress stored?

On the device only, not in the cloud. It does not follow you to a new device unless you restore from a backup, and it is gone if you delete the app.

Does it work offline?

Always. The app never uses the network.

Is anything behind a paywall?

No. There are no in-app purchases and no ads in this version, and growth can only come from training.

Known limits in this version

  • One creature species with three stages of evolution.
  • No iCloud sync, no Apple Health, no Apple Watch, no reminders.

手机放哪

  • 深蹲：手机立在大约膝盖高的位置，离你两步远，正对着你。臀部降到大约膝盖高度再站起来，算一个。
  • 开合跳：和深蹲一样的位置，画面里头顶要留出空间。双手越过头顶、双脚跳开、再并拢回来，算一个。
  • 俯卧撑：手机屏幕朝上平放在身旁地上，离你大约一臂远。先做两个完整的俯卧撑让 App 记住你的幅度，之后每次下到这个深度都算。

常见问题

有一个没算上。

练练宠从不静默漏数。差一点没到位时，屏幕上会直接说原因，比如「Go a little lower」「Jump your feet wider」「Hands all the way over your head」，搭档也会摇头。如果计数还是不对，点「Add one」手动加一，结算页会标出手动加了几个。

一直提示「Step back」或「Too dark」。

App 需要看到你的全身，包括脚。往后退到提示变成「Perfect — hold still」；脚踝被切掉就把手机往下倾一点；开一盏在你前方而不是身后的灯。

我拒绝了摄像头权限。

去 iPhone 的「设置 → Repling → 相机」打开，或者用「Count manually instead」继续手动计数。画面永远只在你的设备上处理。

短片没存进相册。

多半是相册权限关着。在「设置 → Repling → 照片」里打开，或者直接在短片页分享出去，短片都会留在 App 里。

为什么我的搭档和朋友的长得不一样？

你练什么决定它长成什么样。深蹲长腿，开合跳长手臂，俯卧撑长翅膀。练法不同，搭档就不同。

断了几天，它还在吗？

在。它不会死，也不会掉成长值，只会有点蔫，练一次就恢复。

进度存在哪？

只存在本机，不在云端。换新设备时除非从备份恢复，否则不会跟过去；删掉 App 也就没了。

离线能用吗？

一直都能。App 从不联网。

有付费墙吗？

没有。本版没有内购、没有广告，成长值只能靠练出来。
