Repling

You train. It grows.

Prop your phone up and do squats, jumping jacks or push-ups. Repling counts every rep from the camera, and a small creature beside you copies each one. Finish a set and it jumps onto your head to celebrate.

What you train decides what it becomes: squats grow its legs, jumping jacks its arms, push-ups its wings. After a workout you see exactly what changed since yesterday, and get a short vertical clip of your silhouette and your creature to share. Camera frames are processed on your iPhone and thrown away. No account, no ads, no network.

练练宠 Repling

你练，它长。

把手机立好，做深蹲、开合跳或俯卧撑。练练宠用摄像头数你的每一个动作，旁边的小家伙跟着你同步做。练完一组，它会跳到你头上庆祝。

你练什么，它就长成什么样：深蹲长腿，开合跳长手臂，俯卧撑长翅膀。练完能看到它和昨天相比哪里变了，还会剪好一条你的剪影加搭档的竖版短片，可以直接分享。摄像头画面只在这台 iPhone 上处理，用完即丢。不用注册，没有广告，不联网。
