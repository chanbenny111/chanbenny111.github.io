Wrapling Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Wrapling collects no data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stickers and settings are saved on your device. If iCloud is on, Apple's iCloud key-value storage keeps a copy in your own iCloud account so progress follows you between your devices. The developer cannot see it.

Children

Wrapling is rated 4+ and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. Questions: chanbenny111@gmail.com.
