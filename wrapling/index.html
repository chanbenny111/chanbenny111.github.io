Wrapling

Wrap it, carry it, find a way.

Wrapling is a gentle physics puzzle about a little cloth bundle that wraps up whatever it finds. Wrap a stone and you sink; wrap a balloon and you float; drop either one and it stays in the world for you to stand on, push, or press a switch with. Every obstacle has at least two ways through. No account, no ads, no in-app purchases.

小包袱 Wrapling

包起来，带着走，自己想办法过去。

小包袱是一块会包东西的布。包起石头就往下沉，包起气球就飘起来；放下来的东西会留在原地，可以站上去、推着走，或者压住一块压板。每个障碍至少两种过法。不用注册，没有广告，没有内购。
