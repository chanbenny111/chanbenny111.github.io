Wrapling Support

A gentle physics puzzle about a little cloth bundle that wraps up whatever it finds. Email chanbenny111@gmail.com and we'll reply within a few days.

I'm stuck on a level

Every obstacle has at least two ways through. Open Pause and choose "Reset this stretch" to put objects back. The end-of-level card shows how many ways are still waiting.

My progress didn't move to my other device

Progress syncs through your iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on, then open Wrapling on each.

Comfort options

Settings has game speed 0.75x, high contrast, reduce motion, left-handed controls, and switches for haptics, sound and music. Game controllers and hardware keyboards work too.

Privacy

Wrapling collects no data. See the privacy policy.
