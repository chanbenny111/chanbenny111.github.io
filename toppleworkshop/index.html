Topple Workshop

Build the chain. Topple it.

A chain-reaction puzzle for iPhone and iPad. Lay dominoes on a grid, push the first one, and watch the chain ring bells, cross rivers and climb stairs on its way to the score.

万骨连倒：多米诺工坊。设计连锁，推倒，看分数一路涨。

Clients send commissions with a block budget and three stars to earn. The clicks you win buy new block types and bigger maps in your own workshop, and an apprentice rebuilds your blueprint while you are away, for up to 8 hours.

Every layout fits into a short chain code you can send to a friend, and every run can be saved as a 15-second vertical video. The app works fully offline: no account, no ads, no tracking.
