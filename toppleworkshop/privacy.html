数据存在哪 · Where your data lives

在 App 自己的容器里，以及一个只给桌面小组件读的 App Group 容器 （group.com.chanbenny.toppleworkshop）。两处都在你的设备上，随系统备份走， 删除 App 即全部删除。没有服务器，所以也没有「删除我的账号」这件事——没有账号。

Your data lives in the app's own container and in an App Group container read by the home-screen widget. Both are on your device, included in your own backups, and removed entirely when you delete the app. There is no server and no account.

连锁码和导出的视频 · Chain codes and exported videos

连锁码是一串字符，导出的视频是一个写在 App 临时目录里的 15 秒 mp4。两者都交给 系统分享面板，由你决定发给谁或存到哪。App 不读相册，也不申请相册权限， 这两条路都不经过任何服务器。

A chain code is a string of characters; an exported replay is a 15-second mp4 written to the app's temporary directory. Both are handed to the system share sheet, and where they go is your choice. The app never reads your photo library and never asks for photo permission.
