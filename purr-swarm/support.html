The controls, all three of them

  • Drag anywhere to move the red dot. The swarm follows it — you never touch the cats directly.
  • Spread two fingers to fan the swarm out. Wide means better dodging and furballs sprayed in an arc.
  • Pinch to pour the swarm into liquid form. Narrow means squeezing through gaps, firing a focused laser beam, and resisting suction.
  • Hiss with the button in the bottom corner once the floof meter is full. It clears the screen.

Common questions

How do I build floof faster?

Floof comes from grazing — passing close to a projectile without being hit. Playing safe at the edge of the room builds almost nothing. Lean in.

The robot vacuum keeps eating my swarm.

It alternates two phases and the HUD announces each one. Spread out while it sprays dust; pinch tight the moment it starts the intake. Doing it backwards costs about three times as many cats.

I cannot reach the hiss button.

Turn on Settings → Auto-hiss and the hiss fires by itself when the meter fills. Left-handed layout mirrors the controls to the other side.

Can I get a specific coat?

Coats are only found by taking in strays during a run — they are never bought. Keep 60% of the swarm in one coat for that coat's bonus, or get all six coats on the field at once for Full House.

Does it work offline?

Always. The game never uses the network. Airplane mode changes nothing.

Where did my progress go?

Progress lives on the device, not in the cloud, so it does not follow you to a new iPhone and it is gone if you delete the app. Cross-device sync is on the list.

Accessibility

VoiceOver and Dynamic Type are supported throughout, up to the largest accessibility sizes. There is also Reduce flashing (the hiss becomes a spreading ring instead of a flash), Coat shape markers (each coat gets its own shape, not just a colour), Larger HUD, and adjustable drag sensitivity. The game honours the system Reduce Motion setting.

Is anything behind a paywall?

No. There are no in-app purchases, no ads, and nothing locked in this version.

Known limits in this version

  • Two rooms and one boss. The bathroom, bedroom and balcony are planned, each with its own boss.
  • No sound yet — the game is silent on purpose rather than half-done.
  • No Game Center leaderboards, no iCloud sync, no endless mode.

常见问题

炸毛怎么攒得更快？

炸毛来自擦弹 —— 贴着子弹过去又没被打中。躲在房间边上稳着打几乎攒不到，得往前凑。

扫地机器人老是把我的猫吸光。

它在两个阶段之间切换，HUD 会播报当前阶段。喷灰时散开，它开始吸尘的那一刻立即捏紧。反着打大约要多付三倍的猫。

哈气按钮我按不到。

打开「Settings → Auto-hiss」，槽满了会自动放哈气。「Left-handed layout」能把控件镜像到另一侧。

能定向获得某种花色吗？

花色只能在局内收编流浪猫获得，买不到。单一花色占到猫群六成可吃该花色的专属加成；六种花色同时在场则是「全家福」加成。

离线能玩吗？

一直都能。游戏从不联网，开飞行模式没有任何区别。

我的进度去哪了？

进度存在本机，不在云端，所以换新手机不会跟过去，删掉 App 也就没了。跨设备同步在计划中。

无障碍

全程支持 VoiceOver 与动态字体，直到最大的无障碍字号。另有「Reduce flashing」（哈气从闪光改为扩散的环）、「Coat shape markers」（每种花色带自己的形状，不只靠颜色区分）、「Larger HUD」，以及可调的拖动灵敏度。游戏也会遵循系统的「减弱动态效果」设置。

有付费墙吗？
