Purr Swarm

Command 300 cats as the laser dot. Take the house back.

Drag one finger and the swarm follows. Spread your fingers and the cats fan out to dodge and spray furballs; pinch and the whole swarm pours into liquid cat, squeezing through gaps and firing a laser beam. Graze the bullets to fill the floof bar, then hiss and clear the room.

Six to eight minutes a run, one of three catnip upgrades between rooms, a two-phase robot vacuum at the end, and a group photo of the swarm you finished with. No ads, no account, no network.

猫群 Purr Swarm

当激光笔红点，带三百只猫夺回被家电占领的家。

一根手指拖着猫群走。张开手指，猫群散开闪避、散射毛球；捏紧手指，整群猫倒成一道猫液，挤过家具缝并射出激光。贴着子弹擦过去就能攒炸毛，攒满哈一口气清场。

六到八分钟一局，房间之间三选一张猫薄荷升级，结尾是两阶段的扫地机器人 Boss，最后出一张猫群合影。没有广告、不用注册、不用联网。
