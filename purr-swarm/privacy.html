Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Purr Swarm collects nothing. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and the app makes no network connections at all. It runs entirely offline.

What the app stores

Everything the game remembers is written to your device's own storage and never leaves it:

  • Your den progress — fish treats, permanent upgrades, and the coats in your catalog.
  • Your settings — haptics, drag sensitivity, and the accessibility options.

We have no servers, so there is no copy of this data anywhere else. It is not backed up to us, not shared with anyone, and not readable by other apps.

What the app does not do

  • No personal data is collected, not even anonymously.
  • No identifiers are read or created. The app never requests tracking permission and never touches the advertising identifier.
  • No camera, photo library, microphone, location, contacts, notifications, or Game Center access is requested. The app shows no permission prompts.
  • No third-party SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising networks are included.

Sharing a group photo

When you tap Share on the result screen, the app renders the group photo on your device and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes. The app does not upload it anywhere and does not save it to your photo library on its own.

Children

Purr Swarm is safe for children in the sense that matters most here: it collects no data from anyone, of any age, and contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no links out to the web.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress inside the app to clear everything, or delete the app — both remove all of it permanently. There is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the app will ask you first.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日

猫群不收集任何东西。没有账号、没有广告、没有统计、没有追踪，App 完全不联网，全程离线运行。

App 在本机保存什么

游戏记住的一切都写在你设备自己的存储里，不会离开这台设备：

  • 猫窝进度 —— 小鱼干、永久升级、图鉴里收录的花色。
  • 设置项 —— 触感、拖动灵敏度、各项无障碍选项。

我们没有服务器，所以这些数据在别处不存在任何副本：不会同步给我们、不会分享给任何人，其他 App 也读不到。

App 不做的事

  • 不收集任何个人数据，匿名的也不收。
  • 不读取、不生成任何标识符；从不请求追踪授权，也从不接触广告标识符。
  • 不申请相机、相册、麦克风、定位、通讯录、通知与 Game Center 权限，运行期间不会出现任何权限弹窗。
  • 不含任何第三方 SDK、崩溃统计或广告网络。

分享猫群合影

你在结算页点「Share」时，合影在你的设备上渲染完成，然后交给 iOS 系统分享面板，发到哪里由你决定。App 不会把它上传到任何地方，也不会自行存进你的相册。

儿童

猫群对儿童是安全的，最要紧的一点是：它不从任何年龄的任何人那里收集数据，且没有聊天、没有用户生成内容、没有对外网页链接。

删除数据

在 App 内用「Settings → Reset progress」即可清空全部进度；删除 App 同样会永久删除所有数据。我们这边没有任何需要删除的东西。

变更

若将来某个版本确实需要收集什么，本页会在该版本发布之前更新，并且 App 会先征求你的同意。
