常见问题

  • 怎么解锁其他角色？三个目标各对应一个角色：单局收编 20 只猫（宠物医生）、 单局坚持 180 秒（外卖骑手）、击败一次超级吸尘器（猫奶奶）。都可以免费打出来； 买完整版则是立刻拿到这三个。
  • 换了手机 / 重装了 App，买过的东西怎么办？设置页有「恢复购买」。 用原来购买时的 Apple 账户登录，点一下即可。
  • 进度能跨设备同步吗？这一版不能。进度只存在本机，删除 App 会一起删掉。
  • 付费提示会反复弹吗？不会。它只在你第一次打赢吸尘器之后出现一次，关掉就不再出现。
  • 有广告吗？要联网吗？都没有。完全离线可玩，不需要账号。
  • 切后台会丢进度吗？不会。当前这一局会被保存，回到 App 可以接着打。

报告问题时请附上

  • 机型与 iOS 版本（设置 → 通用 → 关于本机）
  • App 版本（设置页底部）
  • 出问题时你在哪一屏、做了什么

FAQ

  • How do I unlock the other characters? Each has one goal: recruit 20 cats in a run (The Vet), survive 180 seconds (Delivery Rider), beat the super vacuum once (Granny). All three can be earned for free; buying the Full Game grants them immediately.
  • New phone or reinstall — what about my purchase? Settings has Restore Purchases. Sign in with the Apple Account you bought it with and tap once.
  • Does progress sync across devices? Not in this version. It lives on the device and is deleted with the app.
  • Will the purchase prompt keep appearing? No. It appears once, after your first vacuum kill, and closes for good.
  • Ads? Internet? Neither. It plays fully offline and needs no account.
  • Do I lose a run when I switch apps? No. The current run is saved and resumes when you come back.

When reporting a problem, please include

  • Device model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • App version (bottom of the in-app Settings screen)
  • Which screen you were on and what you did
