Purrbit 隐私政策

生效日期：2026-10-29 · 适用版本：1.0.0

一句话

Purrbit 不收集你的任何数据。它不联网，没有账号，也没有任何第三方 SDK。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。具体地说：

  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、地址或任何联系方式——App 里没有可以填这些的地方。
  • 不收集位置、通讯录、照片、麦克风或健康数据——App 不请求任何系统权限。
  • 不收集设备标识符或广告标识符，不做任何形式的跨 App 追踪。
  • 不做使用统计、不做崩溃上报、不埋点。

你的游戏进度存在哪里

最佳时间、解锁的角色、购买状态等进度只写在你自己设备上的 App 沙盒里。 它不会被上传，我们也看不到。删除 App 就会连同进度一起删掉。

购买

App 内购买由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。我们不接触、也拿不到你的支付信息。 App 只从系统拿到「这个商品是否已购买」这一个判断，并把它写在本地。

分享

结算页的分享按钮调用的是 iOS 系统分享面板，分享的是你自己设备上生成的截图。 内容不经过我们的服务器——我们没有服务器。

儿童

本 App 不收集任何数据，因此也不会收集儿童的数据。

变更

如果以后的版本改变了数据处理方式，我们会在上架前更新本页面，并在版本更新说明里写清楚。

联系

关于隐私的问题请写信到 chanbenny111@gmail.com。

Purrbit Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-29 · Version 1.0.0

In one line

Purrbit collects nothing. It does not connect to the internet, has no accounts, and contains no third-party SDKs.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No name, email, phone number or address — there is nowhere in the app to enter them.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or health data — the app requests no system permissions.
  • No device or advertising identifiers, and no tracking of any kind across apps.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.

Where your progress lives

Your best time, unlocked characters and purchase state are written only to the app's sandbox on your own device. Nothing is uploaded and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details. The app receives only a yes/no answer about whether a product was purchased, and stores that locally.

Sharing

The share button on the results screen uses the iOS system share sheet and shares a screenshot generated on your device. Nothing passes through our servers — we do not run any.

Children

The app collects no data, and therefore collects no data from children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be stated in the release notes.

Contact

Privacy questions: chanbenny111@gmail.com.
