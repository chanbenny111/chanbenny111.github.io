Common questions

How do I undo a step? Drag back along your line, or tap Undo, to undo one cell at a time. Tap Clear to start the level over.

What counts as three stars? Matching the shortest route, which a solver verified for every level.

Is the daily puzzle the same for everyone? Yes. It is generated on your device from the date, so it needs no connection.

The widget shows an old puzzle. Open the app once. Widgets refresh after the day changes.

Where is my progress stored? Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress. The app has no account and no cloud sync in this version.

常见问题

怎么撤销一步？沿原路拖回，或点「退一格」，每次撤销一格；点「清空重画」可以重新开始这一关。

三星的标准是什么？画出与最短路线一样短的路线，每一关的最短路线都经过求解器验证。

每日一题真的全球同一题吗？是的。题目由设备根据日期生成，所以不需要联网。

小组件显示的是旧的题。打开一次 App 即可；日期变化后小组件会刷新。

进度存在哪里？只存在你的设备上。删除 App 会一并删除进度。本版本没有账号，也没有云同步。
