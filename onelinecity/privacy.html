One Line City · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

One Line City does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, daily streak and settings (sound, haptics, line thickness, Reduce Motion) are saved only on your device. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a small amount of the same data through an App Group shared between the app and its widget, also on your device. None of it is sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, notifications or tracking.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the system share sheet opens with a text result or an image of your metro map. Only you decide where it goes.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it can be used by people of any age.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: chanbenny111@gmail.com.

一笔城 · 隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 11 月 2 日

一笔城不收集、不上传、也不与任何人共享你的个人数据。App 没有账号系统，没有统计分析，没有广告，也没有第三方 SDK，不发起任何网络请求。

留在你设备上的内容

你的关卡进度、星级、每日连续天数和设置（音效、触感、线宽、减弱动效）只保存在你的设备上。主屏幕和锁屏小组件通过 App 与小组件共用的 App Group 读取其中少量同样的数据，同样只在设备内。这些数据不会被发送到任何地方。

权限

App 不申请任何权限：不使用定位、通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风、通知，也不做跨 App 追踪。

分享

点按分享时会打开系统分享面板，内容是文字成绩或你的线路图图片，发到哪里完全由你决定。

儿童

由于 App 不收集任何数据，任何年龄的用户都可以使用。

变更与联系

本政策若有变化，会在此页发布新版本并更新日期。有问题请联系：chanbenny111@gmail.com。
