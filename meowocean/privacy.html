Meow Ocean Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

Meow Ocean ("the app") is a single-player casual game made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is processed when you play and the choices you have. You can reach us at chanbenny111@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

We do not run servers, require accounts, or ask for your name, email, contacts or precise location. Your game progress (levels, coins, cat-fish, settings) is stored only on your device.

"My Cat" photos. If you choose a photo of your cat (or take one) to colour your own cat-fish, the photo is read only on your device to pick colours. It is never uploaded, never sent to us or to any partner, and not stored by the app: only the chosen colours, pattern and name are saved in your game progress. The photo leaves your phone only if you decide to share the "before / after" card yourself through your phone's share sheet. The app uses the system photo picker; on iOS it can only see the photo you pick.

2. Advertising (AppLovin MAX and partner ad networks)

The free version shows ads through the AppLovin MAX mediation platform, which selects an ad from these partner networks: Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, Liftoff (Vungle) and Pangle (and, if enabled, Mintegral). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these companies may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks);
  • Diagnostic data such as SDK crash logs and performance data.

Each partner's privacy policy: AppLovin · Google · Meta · Unity · Liftoff · Pangle · Mintegral.

2a. Game analytics (Google Firebase)

To understand which levels are too hard, which features players use and whether the game runs well, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Remote Config. Firebase processes app interactions (for example "level 12 cleared", "capsule opened"), an app-instance identifier, device and app information (model, OS, language, app version), approximate location derived from the IP address, in-app purchase events and basic error reports. We use this only in aggregate to improve the game and tune how often ads appear; we do not use it to identify you. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland analytics only starts after you consent in the consent form (section 3), and your choice is passed to Firebase through Google Consent Mode. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy · Firebase privacy.

2b. Game Center, challenges and sharing

On iPhone, if you open the Weekly Cup or a leaderboard, the app asks Apple's Game Center to sign you in and submits your Cup and Endless scores to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we do not receive your Apple ID.

"Challenge a friend" creates a short code containing only the ocean, rules and score to beat. Nothing about you is in the code or link. Challenge pages on our website read the code in the link to show it and store nothing.

3. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch we show a consent form (Google's certified Consent Management Platform, IAB TCF v2.2, presented through AppLovin MAX) and share your choice with every partner above. You can accept, reject or customize, and change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • US states with privacy laws (e.g. California, Virginia, Colorado): you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising with the Settings → Do not sell or share my personal info switch. We pass your choice to every ad partner.
  • iOS: we ask for permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and you still get non-personalized ads.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Buying Remove Ads, or an active Meow Club subscription, stops banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain optional and are only shown when you tap a button to watch one.

4. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase happened so the item can be delivered on your device. The optional Meow Club is a monthly auto-renewing subscription: the store tells the app on your device when the current period ends, and you can cancel it any time in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Capsules in the game are never sold: capsule tickets are only earned by playing.

5. Children

Meow Ocean is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We configure our ad networks to serve content suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Progress stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on our side, so there is no account to delete. For data processed by our ad partners, use the links in section 2 or reset your device's advertising ID.

7. Security

All ad and store traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) handled by the partners' SDKs and the app stores.
