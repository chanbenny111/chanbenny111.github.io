2. Virtual items

Coins, capsule tickets, shards, cat-fish and accessories are virtual items with no real-world monetary value. They cannot be exchanged for money, transferred, or sold. Consumable items (coin packs, the Starter Pack) are used once delivered. "Remove Ads" is a one-time, non-consumable purchase you can restore with Restore Purchases on the same store account.

Meow Pass. The premium Meow Pass unlocks the premium rewards of the current two-month season (including tiers already reached). It is a one-season purchase and does not carry over to later seasons. Rewards you reached but did not claim are delivered automatically when the season ends.

Meow Club. The Meow Club is an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings after purchase. Perks (no banner or between-level ads, a daily gift, double aquarium coins, the Gold Crown) last while the subscription is active.

Capsules. Capsules contain random collectibles with the odds shown in the game. They cannot be bought with money: capsule tickets are earned by playing (and one free capsule a day with an optional ad).
