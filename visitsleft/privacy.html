Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your iPhone.

Effective date: October 5, 2026

The short version. Visits Left does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK. The app makes no network requests. Everything you enter stays on your device.

This policy explains how the Visits Left app for iPhone and its widgets (together, “Visits Left” or “the app”) handle information. Visits Left is developed and published by chanbenny, an individual developer (“I”, “me”). If you have questions, write to chanbenny111@gmail.com.

1. Information I collect

None. I do not receive, collect or have access to anything you enter in the app. There are no user accounts and no servers. In App Store privacy terms, Visits Left is “Data Not Collected”.

2. Information stored only on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your iPhone. Your people, visits, plans and Story Jar text live in an App Group container shared only between the app and its own widgets; voice recordings and reaction clips stay in the app’s own private storage and are never shared with the widgets.

  • The people you add: the name or nickname you choose, relationship, age, optional sex, country or region, how often you see them and how long a visit usually lasts, and your guess before the reveal.
  • Your own age, if you choose to enter it.
  • Your visit log: dates, any notes you write and, with Pro, photos you attach. Attached photos are copied into the app’s storage and resized; the original stays in your library untouched.
  • Planned visits: the day you pick for your next visit with someone, shown on Home and in your widgets until then.
  • Story Jar answers: the text you type for a question and, if you record one, the voice recording. Recordings are audio files saved in the app’s own private storage (Application Support/StoryAudio), not in the App Group, so the widgets never see them. They are never uploaded. Deleting an answer, or the person, deletes its recording.
  • Reaction clips: if you turn on “Record my reaction”, a short front-camera video of your face while your number is revealed, with your voice only if you turn on “Include my voice”. It is kept in the app’s temporary folder only until it has been added to your video and shared, until you close the share screen, or until the app is next started, whichever comes first. It is never uploaded.
  • A phone number, if you pick one for a person using the system contact picker.
  • Settings such as reminders and their time, whether you said “Not now” to reminders, plus a cached record of whether Pro is unlocked.

This information never leaves your device through Visits Left. Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) according to your own settings; those backups are handled by Apple and are not accessible to me.

3. Permissions and system pickers

  • Notifications. Requested only when you turn on reminders, never at launch; the app explains why first. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone; no notification server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Microphone. Requested only when you tap the record button in the Story Jar, to record a family member’s answer with their permission, or when you turn on “Include my voice” with “Record my reaction” (off by default). The microphone is used only while you are recording. Recordings stay on your iPhone; a voice with your reaction is mixed into your video and deleted with the clip.
  • Camera. Requested only when you turn on “Record my reaction” (before the reveal, or on the share screen). The front camera films your face while the number is revealed (without sound, unless you also turn on “Include my voice”), and the clip is added picture-in-picture to the video you share. The camera is used only during the reveal, and the clip is deleted as described above. Everything works without it.
  • Calendar. “Save and add to Calendar” opens the system event editor with the visit filled in; the event is saved only if you tap Add, by iOS itself. The app never requests calendar access and never reads your calendars.
  • Photos. Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you select. No access to your photo library is requested.
  • Saving share images. If you choose “Save Image” or “Save Video” from the share sheet, iOS asks for add-only permission so the card or video can be added to your library. The app cannot read your library with this permission.
  • Contacts. Phone numbers are chosen with the system contact picker, which hands the app only the number you tap. No access to your contacts is requested.

4. Sharing

Share cards and share videos are created on your device. They include only what you see in the preview: the name you chose for the person, your guess, the estimate, the Visits Left wordmark, a soundtrack generated by the app and, only if you included it, your reaction clip. Nothing is shared unless you send it yourself through the iOS share sheet, and whatever you share is then governed by the app or service you share it to.

5. Purchases

Visits Left Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. I never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple’s StoreKit on your device to unlock Pro features. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

6. Analytics, advertising and tracking

Visits Left contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash-reporting SDKs and no third-party code of any kind. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier. If you have opted in to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide me with aggregated, anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple’s own terms.

7. Links and email

Links in the app (this policy, support, Terms of Use, the App Store) open in Safari or the App Store, which are covered by Apple’s privacy policy and the policy of the website you visit. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; it sets no cookies and runs no analytics, but GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. If you email support, I receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you. The support inbox is a Gmail account, so Google stores those emails under its own privacy policy. I keep support emails only as long as needed to help you, and you can ask me to delete them at any time.

8. Deleting your data

You are always in control. Delete a person from their detail screen to remove them, their visit log, their photos, their planned visit and their Story Jar answers and recordings. Deleting the app removes all of its data, including widget data, from your device. Data may persist in older device backups until those backups are replaced or deleted.

9. Children

Visits Left is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Since I hold no personal data from the app, there is nothing to access or delete on my side; the only exception is support email, which you can ask me to delete at chanbenny111@gmail.com. I do not sell or share personal information.

11. About the estimate

The number shown in Visits Left is calculated on your device from population statistics (UN World Population Prospects 2024, via Our World in Data, CC BY 4.0). This is a statistical estimate based on population averages, not a prediction about anyone’s health. The app does not collect or infer any health information.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date above. If a change ever meant that data would leave your device, the app would ask for your consent first.
