Frequently asked questions

How is the number calculated?

Visits Left uses a Gompertz–Makeham mortality model, a standard way to describe how mortality changes with age. For each of 237 countries and areas and both sexes, the model is fitted to remaining life expectancy published in the UN World Population Prospects 2024, via Our World in Data (CC BY 4.0).

From that, the app estimates how many more years you and the other person are both likely to be alive (assuming the two are independent), and multiplies it by how often you see each other. If you skip your own age, it uses only theirs. The full method is in Settings → How we calculate.

Is it accurate?

It’s an average, not a forecast. This is a statistical estimate based on population averages, not a prediction about anyone’s health. Real lives vary enormously. The number is meant as a gentle prompt to visit, not as information about any individual.

Why did the number change when I added my age?

With your age, the app counts only the years you’ll both be around, which can be equal to or a little lower than their remaining years alone. It also enables the “time already spent together” line for parents and grandparents.

Why is my number different from a friend’s?

Ages, country or region, the sex option you chose and, most of all, how often you visit all change the result. Try the “what if” slider on a person’s page to see how much visiting more often matters.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro, then go to Settings → Restore Purchases (it’s also on the upgrade screen). Pro is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to renew or cancel. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple handles all payments.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget (or +), search for Visits Left and choose small or medium. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add Visits Left in the widget area. To choose who a widget shows, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget. The free version includes a widget for your first person; Pro adds widgets for everyone.

How do reminders work?

Turn reminders on in Settings or on a person’s page. iOS will then ask for notification permission. Visits Left only nudges you when it has been noticeably longer than your usual rhythm; for people you see weekly or more, you’ll get a gentle Sunday note instead. Logging a visit reschedules everything. With Pro you can choose the time of day.

Not getting reminders? Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Visits Left and make sure notifications are allowed and Focus isn’t silencing them.

How do I delete my data?

All data lives only on your iPhone. To remove one person, open their page, tap Edit, then Delete; their visit log and photos go with them. To erase everything, delete the app. It may still exist in older iPhone backups until those are replaced. Nothing is stored anywhere else, because Visits Left has no servers.

Can I sync between devices?

Not at the moment. Visits Left keeps everything on one iPhone by design, with no account and no cloud service. It is restored along with the rest of your iPhone if you restore from a backup.

Does Visits Left access my contacts or photos?

No. Phone numbers are chosen through the system contact picker and photos through the system photo picker, so the app only ever receives the single number or photo you pick. Saving a share card to Photos asks for add-only permission.

Which languages are supported?

18 languages: English, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish (Spain), Spanish (Latin America, also used in Mexico and the US), German, French (France), French (Canada), Italian, Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Portuguese (Brazil), Arabic and Hebrew. The app follows your iPhone’s language; you can change it per app in iOS Settings → Visits Left → Language.

When you write, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the app).
