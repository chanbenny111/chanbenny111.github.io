How many more times will you see your mom?

Tell Visits Left who matters, how old they are and how often you see each other. In about thirty seconds it shows an honest estimate of the visits you likely have left, so the next one stops feeling like something that can wait.

How it works

Three questions. One number. A reason to go sooner.

  1. Pick a person

    Mom, Dad, Grandma, Grandpa, a partner, an old friend. Give them whatever name you call them.

  2. Tell us how often

    Every week, a few times a year, once a year. Add your own age for a more accurate estimate.

  3. Watch it count down

    The number settles, then becomes a field of dots. One dot for every visit you have left.

It isn’t meant to frighten you. It’s meant to make the next visit feel a little less like something that can wait.

Inspired by Tim Urban’s essay “The Tail End.”

What’s inside

Small tools for staying close

●Every dot is a visit

See the visits already behind you and the ones still ahead. Move the “what if” slider and watch monthly visits add dozens more.

✓“I saw them today”

Log a visit in one tap, add a note, and keep a quiet record of the days you spent together.

☎Call or message, right there

Pick their number with the system contact picker. No access to your address book is ever requested.

☼Gentle reminders

If it has been longer than usual, a soft local notification lets you know. Nothing at launch, nothing pushy.

▦Home & Lock Screen widgets

Keep someone in view: small, medium and Lock Screen widgets that open straight to that person.

↗Videos and cards worth sharing

A short 9:16 video of your reveal with a soundtrack made on your iPhone (add your own reaction if you like), or Story (9:16) and square (1:1) cards. They show only the name you chose.

❖Plan the next visit

Pick a day, get a reminder the evening before, and add it to your calendar without giving the app calendar access.

❝Story Jar

120 questions to ask before it’s too late. Write down the answers, or record them in their own voice, with their permission.

Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion are supported throughout. Available in 18 languages, including Spanish for Spain and Latin America and French for France and Canada.

Private by design

Nothing leaves your iPhone.

No account, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code. Visits Left doesn’t make network requests. Photos come through the system photo picker and phone numbers through the system contact picker, so the app never sees your library or address book. Read the privacy policy.

About the number

Honest math, plainly explained

Visits Left fits a Gompertz–Makeham mortality model to remaining life expectancy from the UN World Population Prospects 2024 (via Our World in Data, CC BY 4.0) for 237 countries and areas and both sexes. When you add your own age, it estimates the years you’ll both be around, not just theirs, and multiplies by how often you visit.

This is a statistical estimate based on population averages, not a prediction about anyone’s health.
